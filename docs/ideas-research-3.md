## A. Moving flat: update your address
1. **Finanças (tax domicile)**: 60 days to report the new tax domicile (gov.pt guide "Mudar de casa"). LGT art. 19(3): communicating domicile is mandatory; 19(4): a domicile change "é ineficaz" until communicated (portal LGT page). Fine: RGIT art. 117(1) punishes failure/late "comunicações" with EUR 150 to 3,750 (RGIT PDF, Portal das Finanças). Art. 117 does not name domicile explicitly: say "can be fined up to", not "will be". Online change only takes effect after entering the code mailed to the new address (Finanças FAQ 00087).
2. **Cartão de Cidadão**: max 60 days after moving (gov.pt service page). Online or at a counter; done only after you confirm with the code in the letter sent to the new address. Cost: UNVERIFIED (page only cites Portaria 291/2017; search snippet said online free / EUR 3 counter, not confirmed on the page).
3. **AIMA residence permit**: deadline and fine UNVERIFIED (aima.gov.pt page failed to open, certificate error). No primary source found for a 60-day rule or fine.
- **One place?** Yes, mostly: changing the CC address is passed automatically to Finanças, Segurança Social and SNS (gov.pt "Mudar de casa"). Not covered: IMT (licence holders without CC), car registration (60 days after confirmation), pet registry SIAC (15 days). Foreigners without CC: no single place found.

## B. SNS número de utente
- Portuguese CC holders already have it (on the back of the card); no request needed (gov.pt service page).
- Foreigners: "any foreign person with legal residence in Portugal can obtain" it; assigned on first visit to a public health unit (centro de saúde or hospital) (gov.pt migrants guide).
- For SNS coverage, number must be linked to: ID document, Portuguese NIF, full address in Portugal, valid residence permit.
- Having the number does not guarantee SNS cost coverage. Without those data the SNS may not cover costs, except cases set by law. Anyone has the right to be seen at a centro de saúde or hospital; emergency 112 or SNS 24 808 24 24 24.

## C. Rent receipts and contract
- Landlords must issue rent receipts (Portal das Finanças, Recibos page). Electronic receipt exemptions: aged 65+ on 31 Dec of prior year; rural leases; or all of: no (and not obliged to have) electronic mailbox AND prior-year rents not above 2x IAS (2026 IAS EUR 537.13, so EUR 1,074.26) and not expected above it this year (FAQ 00356). Exempt landlords not issuing e-receipts file Modelo 44 by end of February of the next year (one FAQ says January: minor conflict, use February from the main page).
- Contract communication: mandatory for landlords, electronically on the Portal, which also settles stamp duty and registers the contract; deadline "até ao fim do mês seguinte ao do início do arrendamento" (FAQ 00015).
