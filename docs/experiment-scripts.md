# Dec 2026 experiment: assignment and scripts

Topics randomly assigned to the 24 runs (seed 20261123). Style for all: the 2D cartoon cast (Otto the expat, Avó the
grandma, Dona the clerk, Buck), narrator + character lines, our own music, 15:00 Lisbon.
Factor rules applied to every script:
- **Hook:** *question* = the narrator asks a question the reel answers; *warning* = "Don't…" / "The mistake…".
- **Length:** 14 s ≈ 32–38 spoken words, 3 beats; 20 s ≈ 48–55 words, 4–5 beats.
- **Content:** *comedy* = the situation and its punchline only; *fact* = the same skit, then one verified fact on screen and
  in narration (≤ 3 s) before the button. Facts are word-for-word from docs/experiment-research/summary.md.
- **Distribution** (trial/normal) is a publishing setting only; it changes nothing in the video.

## Scripts
N = narrator, O = Otto, A = Avó, D = Dona, B = Buck, X = other character. [PT] lines are subtitled in English.

### 08 Dec · Landlord wants cash · fact / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Never pay your rent in an envelope."
- O hands the landlord a fat envelope; landlord counts it twice, licks his thumb.
- O: "Receipt?" Landlord, shocked: "Recibo? [PT] (A receipt?)" and walks off whistling.
- Fact N + card: "Renters deduct 15% of rent, up to €900 for 2026."
- Button: O stares at his empty wallet.

### 09 Dec · Pharmacy queue · comedy / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Never go to a Portuguese pharmacy in a hurry."
- O waits with one box of paracetamol; the lady in front tells the pharmacist about her knee, her son, the neighbour.
- Pharmacist hugs her; she takes a ticket again for "one more thing".
- Button: O's beard has grown; A arrives: "Olá querida! [PT] (Hi, dear!)" and cuts the line.

### 10 Dec · "Only a voucher" · fact / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Don't believe 'only a voucher' at the returns desk."
- O's new toaster launches the toast to the ceiling. He returns it.
- Clerk: "Só vale. [PT] (Only a voucher.)" O: "But it's broken!" Clerk shrugs.
- A steps in with the toaster; toast hits the clerk's head.
- Fact N + card: "Defective goods: 3-year guarantee. Bought online: 14 days to cancel."
- Button: the clerk hands over the refund, toast still on his head.

### 11 Dec · School books · fact / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "Why do Portuguese parents keep every school receipt?"
- September: Buck's kid's book list unrolls to the floor; the till rings €312.
- December: O sees Buck sorting receipts like treasure. "Why?"
- Fact N + card: "School costs: 30% back on your IRS, up to €800."
- Button: O runs back into the shop: "Receipt! With my NIF!"

### 12 Dec · Black Friday "–70%" · comedy / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Don't trust the '–70%' sign on Friday."
- Monday: the TV costs €300. Wednesday: €600. Friday: "–50%! €300!"
- O grabs it, triumphant. A, behind him, slowly shakes her head.
- O: "It's a deal!" A: "Ai, filho… [PT] (Oh, son…)" and shows Monday's flyer.
- Button: O, deflated, still carrying the TV.

### 13 Dec · Bakery weighs the bread · comedy / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "How much is one bread roll in Portugal?"
- O asks for "one pão". The baker weighs it like gold, adds a crumb, removes a crumb.
- Display: €0.17. O pays with a €20 note; the whole shop turns.
- Button: baker [PT] "Não tem trocado? (No change?)"

### 14 Dec · Electricity estimate · fact / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "Why did Otto's electricity bill triple in winter?"
- Bill: "ESTIMATED". O on holiday all month; the meter hasn't moved.
- Fact N + card: "Send your own meter reading. Corrections can't go back over 6 months."
- Button: O photographs the meter like a paparazzo.

### 15 Dec · CTT "we tried to deliver" · comedy / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "How does the CTT deliver a parcel without knocking?"
- O waits at the door all day, eyes on the peephole.
- He blinks once. A note slides under the door: "We tried to deliver."
- At the post office: 47 people in the queue; ticket 312, display 12.
- Button: the parcel is the book "Patience".

### 16 Dec · Condo meeting · comedy / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Never go to your first condomínio meeting."
- Agenda item 1: the hallway light bulb. Hour 1: debate. Hour 2: a vote. Hour 3: recount.
- Button: O, the only one awake, gets elected administrator. A: "Parabéns! [PT] (Congratulations!)"

### 17 Dec · Vet bill · comedy / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Don't take your cat to the vet just for a check-up."
- The cat purrs; the vet smiles; the bill unrolls: check-up, vaccine, "consultation about the consultation".
- O pays; the cat walks out, unimpressed.
- At home the cat knocks the receipt into the bin.
- Button: A to the cat: "Gato de luxo. [PT] (Luxury cat.)"

### 18 Dec · Grandma's raspadinha · fact / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "What happens if grandma wins big on a raspadinha?"
- A scratches with her lucky coin: €10,000! She dances with the café owner.
- Fact N + card: "Santa Casa prizes: 20% tax on the part above €5,000."
- Button: A, unbothered: "Ainda dá para muita bica. [PT] (Still buys a lot of coffee.)"

### 19 Dec · Baptism envelope · fact / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Don't put a cheque in the baptism envelope."
- At the party everyone slips envelopes to the parents; O panics, how much is normal?
- Godfather hands over a giant envelope; aunts whisper.
- Fact N + card: "Gifts to children, parents or grandchildren: 0% stamp duty. Others pay 10%."
- Button: O hands his envelope: a drawing of a baby.

### 20 Dec · Christmas bonus · fact / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Don't spend your Christmas bonus before you see the payslip."
- O books a trip in his head: beach, palm trees, bonus!
- Payslip: smaller. The palm trees deflate.
- Fact N + card: "The Christmas bonus is taxed on its own, never added to your salary."
- Button: O at the beach… of the Tejo, with a bica.

### 21 Dec · No receipt at the café · comedy / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "What happens if you ask for a receipt for one coffee?"
- O: "Fatura, por favor." The café goes silent; the espresso machine stops.
- The barman prints it slowly, it's 1 m long.
- Button: A, at the next table: "Com contribuinte? [PT] (With your tax number?)"

### 22 Dec · Car inspection panic · comedy / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "Why do Portuguese drivers pray before the car inspection?"
- O's old car rattles into the IPO; inspector taps the tyre; a hubcap rolls away.
- Lights test, horn test (it plays a fado note), brakes test.
- Inspector stamps… "Aprovado. [PT] (Passed.)" The car falls apart behind them.
- Button: O drives off in what's left.

### 23 Dec · Bank fees · fact / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "How much should a basic bank account cost a year?"
- O's statement: "maintenance fee", "card fee", "fee for the fee".
- A opens her bank book: almost nothing taken.
- Fact N + card: "A basic bank account costs at most €5.37 a year."
- Button: O at the counter: "Serviços mínimos, por favor. [PT] (The basic one, please.)"

### 24 Dec · Gas bottle guy · fact / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Never miss the gas bottle delivery guy."
- His horn plays in the street; the whole building runs down with empty bottles.
- Fact N + card: "Bottled gas support: €25 back per bottle, until 31 December."
- Button: O arrives late; the van leaves; A waves her full bottle.

### 25 Dec · Splitting the tasca bill · comedy / warning / 14 s
- Hook N: "Never split the bill at a Portuguese family lunch."
- Nine relatives, one bill; everyone grabs it; a tug of war.
- Button: A pays secretly in the kitchen and winks.

### 26 Dec · New puppy · fact / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Got a puppy for Christmas? Don't skip this."
- The puppy wrecks the living room; everyone is in love.
- Neighbour at the door: "Tem chip? [PT] (Does it have a chip?)" O: "A what?"
- Fact N + card: "Microchip and register it within 120 days. Fines up to €3,740."
- Button: the puppy at the vet, proud, wearing a bow.

### 27 Dec · "€1 extra for card" · comedy / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "Why do some cafés stare at you when you pay by card?"
- O taps his card for a €0.80 coffee; the barman sighs, finds the machine in a drawer.
- Button: barman: "Mínimo cinco euros. [PT] (Five euro minimum.)" O orders six coffees.

### 28 Dec · Phone contract · comedy / warning / 20 s
- Hook N: "Never sign a Portuguese phone contract in a hurry."
- O signs; the contract has a ball and chain.
- Months later he tries to leave; the shop assistant laughs, the chain grows.
- He calls customer service: hold music, the same fado, for an hour.
- Button: A, with a phone from 1998: "O meu nunca falha. [PT] (Mine never fails.)"

### 29 Dec · Grandpa pays cash · fact / question / 14 s
- Hook N: "Can grandpa buy a car with a suitcase of cash?"
- Grandpa opens a suitcase of notes at the dealer; the salesman sweats.
- Fact N + card: "Paying €3,000 or more in cash is illegal in Portugal."
- Button: grandpa closes the suitcase: "Então um triciclo. [PT] (A tricycle, then.)"

### 30 Dec · Complaints book · fact / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "What's the most powerful book in Portugal?"
- O's order is wrong for the third time. Calm, he says: "Livro de reclamações."
- The restaurant freezes; the manager runs over with free dessert.
- Fact N + card: "Every shop must have one, paper and online. Fines from €650."
- Button: O eats the dessert, book closed.

### 31 Dec · Proof of address · comedy / question / 20 s
- Hook N: "How do you prove where you live in Portugal?"
- O shows his rental contract: "Not enough." Bills: "Not enough."
- At the Junta, the clerk asks two neighbours to confirm he lives there.
- The neighbours turn out to be A and her friend, who confirm loudly with his whole life story.
- Button: D stamps it: "Atestado. [PT] (Certificate.)" Fireworks: it's New Year.
