# Three life-admin explainers (23 Nov 2026), scripts for approval

**Method (new):** narrated explainer, 25–30 s. One narrator from start to end (free Kokoro voice, English). Motion graphics carry
the information: big kinetic text tied to the words, drawn documents (generic, no logos), arrows and flow diagrams that build
on screen, a checklist that ticks. Otto appears only as a quick cutout reaction (2–3 moments), not as a stage play. Every number
and rule below is from docs/ideas-research-3.md; nothing else is claimed.

---

## 1. Moving flat in Portugal
**Hook (frame 0):** Otto buried in moving boxes, text: "Moved flat in Portugal? Do this ONE thing."

| Time | Narration | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Moved flat in Portugal? Most people update their address four times. You only need once." | Boxes; four building icons pop up (Tax, Social Security, Health, …), each with a red "!" |
| 3–9 s | "Change it on your Cartão de Cidadão, online or at a counter." | A generic citizen card slides in; the address line rewrites itself |
| 9–15 s | "That one change goes to the tax office, Social Security and the health service automatically." | Three arrows shoot from the card to the three icons; each turns green ✓ |
| 15–20 s | "You'll get a letter with a code. Until you enter it, the change doesn't count." | An envelope flies in, opens: a code; a padlock opens when the code is typed |
| 20–25 s | "And you have 60 days. Still separate: your car registration and your pet's registry." | Big "60 DAYS" calendar; two leftover icons (car, paw) with "do these too" |
| 25–28 s | "One change, not four." | Otto relaxing on the boxes; card ✓ |

Not claimed: AIMA rules for foreigners without a citizen card (unverified); the counter fee.

## 2. Rent receipts worth up to €900
**Hook (frame 0):** a hand passing cash, text: "Paying rent in cash? You're losing up to €900."

| Time | Narration | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Paying rent in cash with no receipt? You could be losing up to nine hundred euros." | Cash changes hands; "€900" drops in and cracks |
| 3–9 s | "In Portugal your landlord has to give you rent receipts. Most must issue them electronically, on the tax portal." | A receipt prints out of a laptop; label "electronic rent receipt" |
| 9–14 s | "The lease itself must be registered with the tax office by the end of the month after it starts." | A contract with a stamp; calendar flips to "end of next month" |
| 14–22 s | "Why it matters: tenants get fifteen percent of their rent back on IRS. Up to nine hundred euros for 2026." | Counter: rent €500 × 12 = €6,000 → 15% → €900 (cap) lands in Otto's wallet |
| 22–27 s | "No receipts, no deduction. So ask for them." | Otto holding up a receipt like a trophy |

Example maths uses the verified rate and cap only (15%, €900 for 2026). Not claimed: stamp-duty rate; exemptions on screen
(landlords 65+ and some small landlords don't have to issue electronic receipts, mentioned in the caption only).

## 3. Health number ≠ free healthcare
**Hook (frame 0):** Otto proudly holding a card with a number, text: "Got your SNS number? It might not cover you yet."

| Time | Narration | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Got your SNS health number? It might not cover you yet." | The number glows, then a big "?" |
| 3–9 s | "Any foreign resident can get one, at their first visit to a health centre or hospital." | A health-centre icon; a number gets assigned |
| 9–19 s | "But the health service only covers your costs when the number is linked to four things: your ID, your NIF, your Portuguese address and your residence permit." | A four-slot checklist; each item flies in and ticks ✓ |
| 19–24 s | "Missing one? Sort it out before you need a doctor, not after." | One slot empty, blinking; Otto sweating in a waiting room |
| 24–27 s | "Emergency? Call 112." | "112" big, red |

Not claimed: what you pay without coverage; documents asked at the counter.
