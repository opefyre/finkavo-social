## 9. Rent receipts + IRS rent deduction — PASS (deduction part only)
- On screen: "Renters: deduct 15% of rent in IRS, up to €900 for 2026."
- Sources opened:
  - DL 97/2026 (20 May 2026), art. 15.º n.º 1 (transitional rule) + new CIRS art. 78.º-E n.º 10 — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/decreto-lei-97-2026.pdf
    Quote: "aplicando-se no ano de 2026 o limite de 900,00 €" (€1,000 from 2027).
  - AT leaflet "IRS deduções 2025" — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/Folhetos_informativos/Documents/IRS_deducoes_2025.pdf
    Quote: "Rendas de habitação permanente … 15% do valor suportado"
- Caveats: €900 applies to rent PAID in 2026 (declared in IRS filed 2027); IRS filed in 2026 still uses €700. 15% source is the 2025 leaflet (rule unchanged by DL 97/2026, which only touched n.º 10). Landlord e-receipt obligation NOT verified from a primary source (only secondary) — do not state it on screen, or verify first. Married-separate filing uses 7.5% each.
