## 22. School expenses deduction — PASS
- On screen: "School costs: 30% back on your IRS, up to €800."
- Law: CIRS art. 78.º-D, n.º 1 and 2. https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs78d.aspx
- Quote: "30 % do valor suportado a título de despesas de formação e educação" / "com o limite global de 800 €".
- Includes manuais e livros escolares. Invoices must be on e-fatura, VAT-exempt or reduced rate, CAE 85 (Educação), 47610 (bookshops) or 88910 (childcare).
- Caveats: cap is per household (global), not per child. Page is the current consolidated CIRS; OE2027 could change it from 2027.

## 23. Vet bills VAT deduction — PASS
- On screen: "Vet bill? Get 15% of the VAT back, up to €250."
- Law: CIRS art. 78.º-F, n.º 1 e) and n.º 6. https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs78f.aspx
- Quote: "Secção M, classe 75000 - atividades veterinárias".
- Caveats: €250 cap per household, shared with other 78.º-F sectors (car/moto repair, restaurants/hotels, hairdressers, etc.). Vet medicines: 35% of VAT, same cap. NIF must be on the invoice.
