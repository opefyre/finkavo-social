# Replacement topics B: fact check (23 Nov 2026)

## B1. Estimated electricity bills: PASS
- **On screen:** "Your own meter reading beats the estimate. Corrections can't go back more than 6 months."
- **Source:** ERSE Regulamento de Relações Comerciais (RRC), Reg. 827/2023, consolidated PDF (opened): https://www.erse.pt/media/cw5fdm3i/regulamento-827-2023_consolidado.pdf. ERSE's RRC page lists it as the regulation in force: https://www.erse.pt/atividade/regulamentos-eletricidade/relacoes-comerciais/
  - Art. 36(3): customers may read the meter themselves and send in the reading.
  - Art. 42(3): the latest real reading prevails, "nesta se incluindo a que tenha sido comunicada pelo cliente".
  - Art. 48(5): corrections after estimated bills use real readings, capped at "seis meses".
  - Art. 48(3): a credit owed to the customer is paid on the correction bill itself.
  - Art. 48(7): a large catch-up bill is split into at most 12 monthly instalments (BTN customers).
- **Caveats:** I did not check for RRC amendments after the consolidated version. There is no rule saying "max N estimates". The 6-month cap has an exception for fixed billing plans. In Art. 42(5), the customer chooses the estimation method.

## B2. Livro de Reclamações: PASS
- **On screen:** "Every shop must have a complaint book, paper AND online. Refusing it: up to €90,000 fine."
- **Source:** DL 156/2005, DRE consolidated version (opened): https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2005-34431675
  - Art. 3(1)(a)-(b): the shop must have the book and give it "imediata e gratuitamente" on request.
  - Art. 5.º-B(1): the electronic format is also mandatory.
  - Art. 3(2): having the online book does not excuse a missing paper one.
  - Art. 9: not having or refusing the book is a grave offence. Refusal plus police call-out (art. 3(4)) is very grave.
- **Fines:** RJCE, DL 9/2021 art. 18 (opened): https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/9-2021-155732595
  - Grave: €650–1,500 for an individual, €1,700–3,000 for a micro-company, up to €12,000–24,000 for a large company.
  - Very grave: up to €90,000 for a large company.
- **Caveats:** I opened the original text of DL 9/2021 art. 18, not a consolidated version, so later changes to the amounts are not checked. A safer on-screen line is "fines from €650".

## B3. Price per kg: PASS
- **On screen:** "Sold loose by weight? The shop must show the price per kg."
- **Source:** DL 138/90, wording from DL 162/99 (DRE, opened): https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/162-1999-321723
  - Art. 1(2): goods must also carry "o preço por unidade de medida".
  - Art. 1(3): for loose (granel) goods, only the unit price is shown.
- **Enforcement:** DL 9/2021 art. 22 (opened) makes a breach of art. 1 a grave economic offence, inspected by ASAE.
- **Caveats:** I could not open the DRE consolidated version of DL 138/90. Later amendments (DL 109-G/2021, Lei 10/2023) are not checked. Restaurants and cafés eating on the premises fall under special rules (art. 1(6)). The law says "unidade de medida", not specifically kg.
