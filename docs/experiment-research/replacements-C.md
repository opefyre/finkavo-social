# Replacement topics C: fact check (23 Nov 2026)

## C1. Microchip and SIAC registration for dogs and cats: PASS (with caveat)
- On screen: "Dog or cat? Microchip + SIAC register within 120 days of birth. Fine up to €3,740."
- Law: DL 82/2019, art. 5(1) (deadline) and art. 21(1)(a) (fine). Opened DRE "Legislação Consolidada" export (version dated 14-10-2020), hosted at https://www.jf-esgueira.pt/files/files/upload_697358ef681af5.93663965.pdf. SIAC (https://www.siac.pt/, redirected from siac.vet) confirms the duty for cats, dogs and ferrets.
- Quotes: "deve ser realizada até 120 dias após o seu nascimento" (art. 5); "coima cujo montante mínimo é de (euro) 50 e máximo de (euro) 3740" (art. 21).
- Caveats: the 120 days runs from birth, not acquisition (if birth date unknown: by loss of milk incisors). €44,890 max is for companies. The diariodarepublica.pt page would not render, so the DRE text came from a junta-hosted copy. The consolidated version is dated 2020; amendments after 2020 not checked.

## C2. Estimated water bills / meter readings: FAIL
- Could not open a primary source. The diariodarepublica.pt page for DL 194/2009 did not render, and no ERSAR regulation text opened.
- Secondary sources disagree. The 2015 ERSAR guide and DL 194/2009 art. 67(2) say twice a year, max 8 months apart. ERSAR Regulamento 594/2018 art. 92 is cited as max 6 months. Not verified, so drop.

## C3. Atestado de residência from the Junta: PASS
- On screen: "Proof of address in Portugal? Your Junta issues it. Avenidas Novas charges €6."
- Source: Regulamento n.º 230/2024, Freguesia de Avenidas Novas, DR 2.ª série n.º 38, 22 Feb 2024, Anexo I, cap. I, item 1. Opened the file at https://www.jf-avenidasnovas.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Aviso-DR.pdf.
- Quote: "Atestados, Declarações e Certidões para qualquer finalidade ... Por documento 6,00 €".
- Fees set per junta: the regulation sets its own formula, TSA = (Tme × Vh) + (Ct/N). Other juntas' tables were not opened.
- Caveats: the €6 is from the 2024 regulation. Annual updates for 2026 were not checked, though the file is hosted in a 2026 folder. The regulation exempts some uses (e.g. military, job centre). No primary source opened for "what it's for" beyond "any purpose"; do not claim specific uses (e.g. AIMA) on screen.
