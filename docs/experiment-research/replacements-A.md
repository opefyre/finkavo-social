# Replacement topics A (checked 23 Nov 2026)

## A1. Cash payment limit — PASS
- On screen: "Paying €3,000 or more in cash is illegal in Portugal."
- Law: Lei Geral Tributária art. 63.º-E (added by Lei 92/2017). Opened: https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?artigo_id=253A0063E&nid=253&tabela=leis
- Quote: "É proibido pagar ou receber em numerário" (transactions ≥ €3,000).
- Caveats: the limit is €10,000 for individuals not resident in Portugal who are not acting as a business. Taxes cannot be paid in cash above €500. Splitting one sale into smaller payments still counts. Exceptions: dealings with banks/payment institutions/currency exchange, and court-ordered payments. Businesses must pay invoices of €1,000 or more by a traceable method. The Portal das Finanças LGT page URL returned 404, so only the PGDL mirror was opened.

## A2. Basic bank account fee cap — PASS
- On screen: "A basic bank account costs at most €5.37 a year in 2026."
- Law: DL 27-C/2000 (serviços mínimos bancários), cap = 1% of IAS. Opened: Banco de Portugal brochure (June 2026), https://clientebancario.bportugal.pt/sites/default/files/2026-06/BrochuraSMB.pdf
- Quote: "um por cento do valor do indexante dos apoios sociais (IAS), ou seja, 5,37 euros"
- Caveats: the cap covers all fees for the included services combined, per year. Extra services outside the package can cost more. The law text itself was not opened (the clientebancario HTML pages returned 403); the BdP brochure was the source.
