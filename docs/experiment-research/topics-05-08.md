# Topics 5–8 fact-check (23 Nov 2026)

## 5. Pharmacy purchases = IRS health expense — OK
- On screen: "Pharmacy receipts give you back 15% on IRS, up to €1,000."
- Source: CIRS art. 78.º-C n.º 1, a), d), n.º 7 — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs78c.aspx
- Quote: "15 % do valor suportado a título de despesas de saúde"
- Off screen: €1,000 is the household global cap. Article says "exempt or reduced VAT" and "normal VAT with receita médica" (normal-rate invoices must be flagged on the Portal) — it does NOT name 6%/23%; don't put rates on screen unless sourced from the IVA code. Insurer-reimbursed parts excluded.

## 6. CTT parcel pickup / non-EU customs — OK (pickup); customs fee OK with caveat
- On screen: "Missed a parcel? CTT holds it 10 working days, then it goes back."
- Source: CTT "Prazo para levantar" — https://www.ctt.pt/ajuda/particulares/receber/levantar-correspondencia-ou-encomendas-/prazo-para-levantar
- Quote: "começa a contar a partir do dia útil seguinte à data do aviso"
- Table: national parcel 10 working days, letters/registered 6, international parcel 15, Expresso national 5.
- Customs (seen only in search snippets of ctt.pt "Serviços cobrados", page not opened): CTT clearance €7+VAT (≤€150, VAT not paid at checkout), €14 for €150–1000; EU duty exemption ≤€150 ended 1 Jul 2026; +€2+VAT handling fee per item type from 1 Nov 2026. Open https://www.ctt.pt/ajuda/particulares/importacoes-e-desalfandegamento/taxas-e-servicos-cobrados/servicos-cobrados before using these numbers.

## 7. Telecom fidelização early exit — OK
- On screen: "Leaving your phone contract early? Max penalty: 50% of what's left (year 1)."
- Source: Lei 16/2022 (LCE) art. 136.º n.º 4 — https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?artigo_id=3560A0133&nid=3560&tabela=leis&pagina=1&ficha=1 (consolidated: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2022-187527517, not opened)
- Quote: "não podem exceder o menor dos seguintes valores"
- Off screen: lower of (benefit pro-rata to remaining period) or 50% of remaining monthly fees in year 1 / 30% in year 2; 30% in a renewed fidelização without new line. Max fidelização 24 months (search snippet only). Secondary mirror (PGDL), not DRE itself.

## 8. Condomínio + fundo comum de reserva — OK (owner/reserve); tenant part unverified
- On screen: "Own a flat? At least 10% of your condo fee goes to a mandatory reserve fund."
- Sources: DL 268/94 art. 4.º n.º 1–2 (as amended by Lei 8/2022) — https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/1994-144575382-177443438 ; CC art. 1424.º n.º 1 — https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/1966-34509075-49924375 (both via search extracts; DRE page bodies did not load in fetch)
- Quote: "pelo menos, 10 % da sua quota -parte nas restantes despesas"
- Off screen: 10% is of the owner's share of other expenses, not literally "of the quota". Fund must be in a bank; reused money repaid within 12 months. CC 1424: owners pay pro rata to fraction value. "Tenants don't pay unless agreed" (CC art. 1078) NOT verified — keep off screen.
