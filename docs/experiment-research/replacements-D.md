# Replacements D: fact check (23 Nov 2026)

## D1. Card surcharges: PASS (with one caveat)

- **On screen:** "Paying by card in Portugal? The shop can't charge you €1 extra."
- **Law:** RJSPME (annex to DL 91/2018), art. 101.º n.º 7. The ban covers cards whose interchange fees are regulated (Reg. (UE) 2015/751, ch. II), which means ordinary consumer debit and credit cards.
- **Opened:** official DR PDF https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2018/11/21700/0521105260.pdf
- **Quote:** "o beneficiário não pode cobrar encargos pela utilização de instrumentos de pagamento"
- **Still current?** I opened the 4th amendment, Lei 68/2025 (https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2025/12/24400/0000500014.pdf). It changes arts. 2, 38, 52, 68, 149-151 and 153, not art. 101.
- **Caveats:**
  - The consolidated DR page would not load (JS only), so the 2nd and 3rd amendments were not checked.
  - The ban does not cover cards outside Reg. 2015/751, such as commercial or corporate cards and three-party schemes like Amex.
  - The "€1" is only an example. Art. 101 sets no amount.
  - DL 3/2010 (POS charges) was not opened.
  - Banco de Portugal pages refused automated access (403).

## D2. Invoice for every sale + NIF on request: PASS

- **On screen:** "Every sale needs a fatura, even €1, and your NIF if you ask."
- **Law:** Código do IVA art. 29.º n.º 1 b) (invoice for every sale) and art. 36.º n.º 16 (NIF when the buyer asks). Art. 36.º n.º 16 was added by DL 28/2019.
- **Opened:**
  - https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva29.aspx
  - https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/civa_rep/Pages/iva36.aspx
- **Quotes:**
  - Art. 29: "Emitir obrigatoriamente uma fatura por cada transmissão de bens ou prestação de serviços"
  - Same article: "ainda que estes não a solicitem"
  - Art. 36 n.º 16: "é sempre obrigatória quando este o solicite"
- **Caveats:**
  - The AT pages list amendments up to DL 49/2025.
  - The exceptions in art. 29 (e.g. some VAT-exempt activities) were not checked. Say "shops", not "everyone".
  - A simplified invoice or fatura-recibo also counts as a fatura.
  - The "€1" is an example. The law has no minimum.
