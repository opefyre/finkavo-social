# Topics 01–04 fact-check (23 Nov 2026)

## 1. Subsídio de Natal withholding — OK
- On screen: "Your Christmas bonus is taxed on its own, never added to your salary."
- Source: CIRS art. 99.º-C n.º 5–6 — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs99c.aspx
- Quote: "sempre objeto de retenção autónoma"
- Caveats (keep out): split payments withhold proportionally (n.º 6). Despacho 12523-A/2026 adjusted November 2026 tables (I could not read its body text), so give no rate or amount. No number verified beyond the rule.

## 2. Raspadinha / jogos sociais prizes — OK
- On screen: "Win over €5,000 on a Santa Casa game? 20% of the extra goes to tax."
- Source: Tabela Geral do Imposto do Selo, verba 11.4 (Lei 42/2016) — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/ccod-selo-tabgiselo.aspx
- Quote: "sobre a parcela do prémio que exceder € 5.000" — 20%
- Caveats: it is only on the part above €5,000 (not the whole prize). Verba 11.3 (4.5%) is on the bet price, not the prize.

## 3. Euromilhões prize tax — OK (same rule as 2)
- On screen: "Euromilhões jackpot? 20% stamp duty on everything above €5,000."
- Source/quote: same verba 11.4 as topic 2.
- Caveats: "withheld at payment / who pays" NOT verified from the Code articles; do not claim it on screen. Euromilhões counted as a jogo social do Estado is from general knowledge, not from the page I opened. Overlaps topic 2; pick one.

## 4. Restaurant receipts IVA deduction — OK for the deduction; FAIL for the "only the NIF on the invoice" claim
- On screen: "Restaurant bills: 15% of the VAT back off your IRS, household cap €250."
- Source: CIRS art. 78.º-F n.º 1 c) and n.º 2 (last amended list headed by Lei 73-A/2025) — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs78f.aspx
- Quote: "€ 250 por agregado familiar"
- Caveats: the €250 cap is per household and shared with other categories (car repair, hairdresser, vets, etc.). The article says VAT "suportado por qualquer membro do agregado"; it does not say only the NIF-holder gets it, so don't make that claim (the NIF angle needs its own source).
