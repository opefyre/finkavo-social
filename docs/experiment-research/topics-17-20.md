## 19. Bottled gas support (Botija/Bilha Solidária) — PASS (expires soon)
- On-screen: "Bottled gas: €25 back per bottle, up to 2 a month."
- Source: ERSE, Tarifas solidárias (page updated 1 Oct 2026), https://www.erse.pt/combustiveis-e-gpl/tarifas-solidarias/
- Legal basis (per ERSE): Despacho 1016/2026 (regulation, €15); Despacho 3847-A/2026 (raised to €25); Despacho 8400-A/2026 and RCM 191/2026 (extensions; 2026 budget €5,745,596).
- Quote: "até 31 de dezembro de 2026, ou até ao esgotamento da dotação disponível"
- Who: TSEE (social electricity tariff) customers, or households with a listed minimum social benefit. Limit 12 bottles a year. Claimed through participating juntas de freguesia.
- Caveats: ends 31 Dec 2026, so it must air before then. The Despacho pages on DRE did not render, so the despachos were seen only through ERSE. Nothing found for 2027.
