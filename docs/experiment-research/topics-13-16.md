# Topics 13–16 fact-check (23 Nov 2026)

## 13. Cash gifts between family (Imposto do Selo) — PASS
- On-screen: "Gift to your kid? 10% stamp duty… except family pays 0%."
- Rate: TGIS verba 1.2, 10% — "Aquisição gratuita de bens, incluindo por usucapião" — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/ccod-selo-tabgiselo.aspx
- Exemption: CIS art. 6.º n.º 1 e) — "O cônjuge ou unido de facto, descendentes e ascendentes" — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/selo6.aspx
- Out of scope: CIS art. 1.º n.º 5 d) "Donativos conforme os usos sociais … até ao montante de (euro) 500"; g) family cash gifts up to €5,000 — https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/selo1.aspx
- Caveats: "usos sociais" exclusion is capped at €500 (not unlimited). Family gifts above €5,000 are exempt but still must be declared (exemption ≠ no paperwork; declaration duty not separately opened). Grandparents/grandchildren covered as ascendants/descendants. Siblings, uncles, friends pay 10%.
