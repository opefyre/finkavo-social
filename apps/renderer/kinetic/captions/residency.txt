Spending more than 183 nights a year in Portugal can make you a Portuguese tax resident — even if you never planned to be one.

How Portugal decides tax residence (IRS):

· More than 183 days, in a row or not, in any 12-month period that starts or ends in the tax year
· A day counts if it includes a night slept in Portugal — even part of a day
· Or, with fewer days: having a home in Portugal on any day of that period, in conditions that suggest you mean to keep it and live in it as your usual residence

Why it matters: residents are taxed in Portugal on their worldwide income. Non-residents are taxed only on income from Portuguese sources.

What to do: count your nights. If you meet either test, tell Finanças within 60 days and update your details. For a foreign national whose registered address is still abroad, that change is not covered by the Portal's self-service address form — use e-balcão, an appointment, or a Loja do Cidadão.

Special situations, such as a tax treaty or a part-year stay, can change the answer.

Source: Código do IRS, art. 15.º and 16.º; Lei Geral Tributária, art. 19.º n.º 5; Portal das Finanças.
