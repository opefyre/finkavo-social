Finished renovating a property in Portugal? Finanças has to be told within 60 days, because the works may change the property's tax value (VPT) — the figure IMI is charged on.

What the law says (Código do IMI, art. 13.º):

· Within 60 days of finishing building, improvement or other works that may change the VPT, the owner must declare it
· The 60 days count from the conclusion of the works
· IMI is charged each year on the VPT registered on 31 December
· Late or missing declarations can bring a fine of €150 to €3.750 (RGIT, arts. 116.º and 117.º)

What to do: file the Modelo 1 do IMI on the Portal das Finanças (IMI → Modelo 1 → Entregar Declaração) and add the plans of the works. If the works were licensed, the form asks for the licence date; without a licence, the completion date.

Not every small repair counts — the rule is about works that may change the value. The person who files is the owner (or the usufructuary or holder of the surface right).

Source: CIMI art. 13.º, 37.º and 113.º; RGIT art. 116.º and 117.º; Portal das Finanças, Modelo 1 instructions.
