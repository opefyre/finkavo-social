In Portugal you cannot pay or receive €3.000 or more in cash — and splitting the payment does not get around it.

The rule (Lei Geral Tributária, art. 63.º-E):

· Cash is banned for transactions of €3.000 or more — exactly €3.000 is already too much
· It covers transactions of any kind, including a sale between two private people, and both paying and receiving
· Payments linked to one sale are added together, even if each is under the limit: two payments of €2.000 for a €4.000 car still break the rule
· Non-resident individuals who are not acting as businesses have a higher limit: €10.000

The fine for a cash transaction above the limit is €180 to €4.500 (RGIT, art. 129.º n.º 3).

What to do: move the money by bank transfer and keep the confirmation.

Two other cash rules get mixed up with this one: businesses must pay invoices of €1.000 or more by a traceable method, and taxes above €500 cannot be paid in cash.

Source: LGT art. 63.º-C and 63.º-E; RGIT art. 129.º; Portal das Finanças.
