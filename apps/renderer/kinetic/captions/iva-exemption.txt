Freelancers on Portugal's IVA exemption: there are two limits, and the second one applies in the middle of the year.

The small-business exemption (CIVA, art. 53.º) currently works like this:

· €15.000 — if your turnover last calendar year was €15.000 or less, you charge no IVA this year. If it was above €15.000, normal IVA applies from 1 January
· €18.750 (the limit plus 25%) — if your turnover this year passes it, IVA applies at once, from the invoice that takes you over. It does not wait for next year

Turnover here is net of IVA and leaves out sales of business assets.

What to do: keep a running total as you invoice. If you pass a limit, file a change declaration (declaração de alterações) on the Portal das Finanças within 15 working days — counted from the end of the year in the first case, and from the date of the invoice that crossed €18.750 in the second.

The limit has been €15.000 since the 2025 change (Decreto-Lei 35/2025). While you are exempt you also cannot deduct the IVA on your own purchases.

Source: CIVA art. 53.º and 58.º; Ofício-Circulado 25062/2025; Portal das Finanças.
