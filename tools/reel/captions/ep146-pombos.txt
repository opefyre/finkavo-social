Feeding pigeons in Lisbon can cost you €1,000. 🐦🥖

Lisbon's urban hygiene rules (Aviso n.º 20811-B/2019, art. 84.º n.º 2 a)) make it an offence to scatter food on streets and other public spaces that is likely to attract stray animals, naming dogs, cats and pigeons. The fine is €50 to €1,000 for individuals, halved if it's by negligence (art. 86.º). The council can authorise exceptions. Other cities have their own rules.
