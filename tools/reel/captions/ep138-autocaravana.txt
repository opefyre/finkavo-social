Sleeping in your van in Portugal? Careful. 🚐🌊 ("Aqui pode" = "Here you can")

Motorhomes can't overnight (with people inside, 22:00 to 07:00) in Natura 2000, protected areas or coastal-plan zones except in authorised places. Fine: €120 to €600 there, €60 to €300 elsewhere. Outside those areas, the limit is 48 hours in the same municipality unless the council has its own rules (Código da Estrada, art. 50.º-A).
