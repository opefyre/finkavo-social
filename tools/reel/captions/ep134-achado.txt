Found €50 in Portugal. Can you keep it? 💶🚓 ("Volte daqui a um ano" = "Come back in a year")

Not straight away. In Portugal you have to announce what you found and hand it to the police (PSP or GNR). If the owner doesn't claim it within a year, it becomes yours (Código Civil, art. 1323). There's no finder's reward, only your expenses. Keeping it can be a crime (Código Penal, art. 209).
