In Portugal, your weeds are the council's business. 🐐🔥 ("Eu tenho as cabras há quarenta anos" = "I've had the goats for 40 years")

Fire prevention law: owners must clear the vegetation in a strip of at least 50 metres around their buildings (DL 124/2006, art. 15; DL 82/2021, art. 49). If you don't, the câmara municipal (town council) can do it for you and charge you for it. Deadlines and fines vary, so check with your council.
