Under 24 in Portugal? Your transport pass may be free. 🚌🎒

Young people up to 23 (inclusive), students or not, can get their region's monthly public transport pass for free: the sub18 pass from 4 to 18 and the sub23 pass from 18 to 23, valid until the end of the month you turn 24 (Portaria 307-A/2024/1; still running per the government in July 2026). In Lisbon you activate it in the navegante app; in Porto you apply with Andante. Other regions: ask your local operator. It doesn't apply to CP's green rail pass.
