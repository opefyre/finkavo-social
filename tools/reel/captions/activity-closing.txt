Stopped working as a freelancer in Portugal? You have 30 days to tell Finanças. The declaration is called cessação de atividade.

What the law says:

· Deadline: 30 days from the date you stopped (CIVA art. 33.º; CIRS art. 112.º n.º 3)
· Late or missing: a fine of €300 to €7.500 (RGIT, art. 117.º n.º 2)
· For an individual, closing the IVA and the IRS activity go together

Social Security: there is no separate closing step. Segurança Social is told by Finanças, and the contribution obligation ends on the first day of the month after the activity stops. A quarterly declaration may still be due at the next declaration date.

What to do: file the cessação de atividade on the Portal das Finanças (or at any Finanças office or Loja do Cidadão) within 30 days of your last day of work.

Source: CIVA art. 33.º; CIRS art. 112.º; RGIT art. 117.º; Segurança Social, Guia Prático 1009; Portal das Finanças.
