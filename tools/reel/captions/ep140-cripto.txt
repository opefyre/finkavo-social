Sell crypto in Portugal after a year? Tax: €0. 📈⏳

Gains on crypto held for 365 days or more aren't taxed (Código do IRS, art. 10.º), unless the other side of the deal is outside the EU/EEA or a country Portugal has a tax agreement with. Held for less: 28%, or you can choose to add the gain to your other income. Swapping one coin for another isn't taxed at the swap; the tax waits for the sale. NFTs and professional trading follow other rules.
