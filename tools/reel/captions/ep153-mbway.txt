Your bank can't charge you for these transfers. 📱💸

Portuguese law (Decreto-Lei 3/2010, art. 3.º-A, added by Lei 53/2020) bans fees for consumers on transfers made through payment apps like MB WAY that don't exceed €30 per transfer, €150 a month or 25 transfers a month. The law lists the three limits with "or", so the safe reading is to stay under all three. Above them, fees are capped by law. Check your bank's price list.
