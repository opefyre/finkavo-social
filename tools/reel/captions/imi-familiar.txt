Have children? Your IMI can drop by up to €140 a year — if your município has approved the discount.

IMI Familiar (Código do IMI, art. 112.º-A):

· Each município decides whether to apply it
· The amounts are set by law: €30 for 1 dependant, €70 for 2, €140 for 3 or more
· It applies to your own permanent home (the household's tax address must be there)
· The AT applies it automatically, based on your household as it stood on 31 December of the previous year

Some municípios apply only some of the tiers, and some do not apply it at all.

What to do: on the Portal das Finanças, search "dedução fixa para agregados familiares" and check your município and the year. Make sure your dependants are in your IRS household and your tax address is the home.

Source: CIMI art. 112.º-A (Lei 56/2023); Portal das Finanças.
