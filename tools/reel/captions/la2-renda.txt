Paying rent in cash? You could be losing up to €900. 💶🧾

In Portugal your landlord has to give you rent receipts, and most must issue them electronically on the Portal das Finanças (some landlords, like those over 65, are exempt from the electronic version). The lease must be registered with the tax office by the end of the month after it starts. Why it matters: tenants deduct 15% of their rent on IRS, up to €900 for rent paid in 2026. No receipts, no deduction.
