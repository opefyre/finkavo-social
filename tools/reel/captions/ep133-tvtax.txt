Why do the Portuguese pay for TV… on their electricity bill? 📺⚡ ("Pago a televisão e nem a ligo" = "I pay for the TV and never even switch it on")

Look for "Contribuição Audiovisual" on your electricity bill. It's a levy charged on the electricity supply, not on owning a TV (Lei 30/2003), and it goes to RTP, the public broadcaster. No TV? You still pay it.

Did you know it was on your bill? 👇
