Selling online? Platforms report some sellers to Finanças — but the report is not a tax.

How it works (EU directive DAC7, in Portuguese law since Lei 36/2023):

· The platform sends the report to the Autoridade Tributária, not you — once a year, by 31 January
· For goods, a seller is reported at 30 or more sales, or more than €2.000, in a year
· Hosts who rent out property (Airbnb-style) are reported without that threshold
· The AT states that the reporting creates no new tax

Selling things you own is different from trading. Buying in order to resell regularly is business activity (IRS category B), and that has to be opened at Finanças.

What to do: if you buy to resell regularly, open the activity. If you sell your own used things now and then, you are not trading.

Source: Lei 36/2023; Portal das Finanças FAQs on DAC7; CIRS art. 3.º.
