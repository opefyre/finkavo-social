Can a haircut lower your taxes in Portugal? 💈🧾 ("Contribuinte?" = "Your tax number?")

Yes. Give your NIF and 15% of the VAT on hairdressers, restaurants, car and motorbike repairs and vets counts against your IRS. Monthly public-transport passes: 100% of the VAT. Up to €250 a year per household (CIRS, article 78.º-F).
