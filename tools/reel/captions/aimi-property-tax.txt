IMI is not the only property tax in Portugal. Above a threshold, there is a second one: AIMI (Adicional ao IMI).

How it's built (CIMI art. 135.º-B, 135.º-C, 135.º-F):

· It sums the taxable value (VPT) of the urban housing and building land you own, as of 1 January
· For individuals, the first €600.000 of that total is deducted automatically — no application needed
· Above the deduction, the rate is 0,7% up to €1.000.000, 1% on the next tranche up to €2.000.000, and 1,5% above that

Commercial, industrial, service-use and rural property fall outside AIMI's scope entirely — this reel is about housing and building land.

Finanças assesses AIMI itself in June from its own records, and payment is due in a single instalment in September. There is no separate return for most individual owners.

What to do: check the total taxable value of your properties under "Imóveis" on the Portal das Finanças before June, so the September bill isn't a surprise.

Hedges: married or de-facto couples can elect joint taxation to raise the deduction to €1.200.000 combined, but we did not confirm this figure against a primary source cleanly enough to put it on screen — treat it as likely but unverified until you check with Finanças. Companies and undivided estates follow different rates, not covered here.

Source: CIMI art. 135.º-B, 135.º-C, 135.º-F (Portal das Finanças).
