In Portugal, your neighbour's party has a legal bedtime. 🎉🚓🌙

Under Portugal's general noise regulation (Regulamento Geral do Ruído, art. 24.º), when neighbourhood noise happens between 23:00 and 07:00, the police can order whoever is making it to stop immediately. During the day they can also act, but they set a deadline instead. Ignoring the order is an offence, and the town council handles the fine. Some councils add their own local rules.
