Getting married in Portugal costs… how much? 💍🐷 ("O casamento é barato. A festa é que não." = "The wedding is cheap. The party isn't.")

At the Conservatória do Registo Civil, the marriage process and registration cost €120 (Regulamento Emolumentar dos Registos e Notariado, art. 18). A ceremony outside the registry, out of hours, or on a weekend or holiday is €200 instead. The party total in the video is a joke, not a statistic.
