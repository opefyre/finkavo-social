Your Portuguese driving licence has an expiry age. 🚗👴

In Portugal, a category B (car) licence is renewed every 15 years until 60, then at 60, 65 and 70, and every 2 years after 70 (Regulamento da Habilitação Legal para Conduzir, arts. 16.º and 17.º). From 60, renewal needs a medical certificate. Licences issued up to 1 January 2013 follow the older schedule and also renew at 50 (gov.pt). You can renew in the 6 months before it expires.
