Why Portuguese workers get paid in lunch money. 🍽️💶

The meal allowance (subsídio de refeição) is free of income tax up to a daily limit: €6.15 a day in cash in 2026 (Portaria n.º 51-B/2026/1), or 70% more, about €10.46, when it's paid in meal vouchers (Código do IRS, art. 2.º n.º 3 b) 2)). Only the part above the limit is taxed, like salary. That's why so many employers pay it on a meal card.
