Finanças will never ask you to pay through a link in an email or SMS. If a message says it will, do not click.

What the Autoridade Tributária says about how it contacts you:

· It only sends emails from addresses ending in @at.gov.pt
· It never sends emails or SMS with links to enter, change or confirm your personal or tax data
· It never asks for payment through links sent by email or SMS

Fake messages usually create urgency — "pay in 24 hours", "last notice" — so you react before you check.

What to do: do not click. Open the Portal das Finanças yourself (type the address, do not use the link) and check Comunicações after you log in. If it is real, it will be there.

Source: Portal das Finanças security alerts, 16 April 2025 and 3 August 2026.
