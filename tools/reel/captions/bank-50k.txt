Your bank tells Finanças when your balance passes €50.000 — once a year, for residents.

The rule (Decreto-Lei 64/2016, art. 10.º-A, added by Lei 17/2019):

· Banks report, by 31 July each year, accounts whose balance or value at 31 December of the previous year is above €50.000
· It covers accounts held by people resident in Portugal
· It is an annual report, not a daily view of your money

Finanças cannot simply open your bank data: access without your consent is allowed only in the cases listed in the Lei Geral Tributária (art. 63.º-B), with a reasoned written decision.

If you live in Portugal and hold an account abroad, you have your own duty: declare it in your IRS return, Anexo J, quadro 11 — even if it earned nothing.

Source: DL 64/2016 art. 10.º-A; LGT art. 63.º-B; Modelo 3 Anexo J instructions.
