Shaking a rug out of your window can cost €1,000 in Lisbon. 🪟💨

Lisbon's urban hygiene rules (Aviso n.º 20811-B/2019, art. 84.º n.º 2 g)) make it an offence to shake or beat blankets, doormats, rugs, carpets or clothes out of windows and doors that face the street between 8:00 and 23:00. The fine is €50 to €1,000 for individuals, halved if it's by negligence. The same rules fine laundry that drips onto the street. Other cities have their own rules.
