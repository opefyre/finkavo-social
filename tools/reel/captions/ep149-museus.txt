Live in Portugal? Museums can be free for you. 🏛️🎟️

Since August 2024, people who live in Portugal get 52 free days a year at the 37 national museums, monuments and palaces run by Museus e Monumentos de Portugal, such as the Torre de Belém. Pick any days you like and just show your ID at the ticket desk. Sources: gov.pt and museusemonumentos.pt.
