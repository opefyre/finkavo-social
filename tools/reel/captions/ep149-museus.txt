Live in Portugal? Museums can be free for you. 🏛️🎟️

Since August 2024, people who live in Portugal, including foreign residents with a NIF, get 52 free days a year at the 37 national museums, monuments and palaces run by Museus e Monumentos de Portugal, such as the Torre de Belém. Pick any days you like and show your ID and tax number (NIF) at the ticket desk. Sources: gov.pt and museusemonumentos.pt. Rules can change, so check before you go.
