Can you name your baby Khaleesi in Portugal? 👶📋 ("Seis palavras. Escolha." = "Six words. Choose.")

Yes — Khaleesi, Nirvana, Kevin and Elvis are on the IRN's list of admitted first names. But a full name can have at most six words (usually two first names and four surnames), and names must be Portuguese or adapted unless the child or a parent is foreign.
