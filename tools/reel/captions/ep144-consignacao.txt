Portugal lets you give away tax you already paid. 🧾❤️

In your IRS return (or beforehand on the Portal das Finanças), you can send 1% of the income tax assessed on you to a charity (IPSS), a religious community, or a cultural, youth or sports organisation on the tax office's official list (Lei 16/2001, art. 32.º, and Código do IRS, art. 152.º, at 1% since income earned in 2024). The 1% comes out of the tax you already owe, so your bill doesn't go up. A separate option lets you give away your IVA deduction too, but that one does cost you the deduction.
