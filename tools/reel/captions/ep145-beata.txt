This tiny thing can cost you €250 in Portugal. 🚬💶

Since September 2020, dropping a cigarette or cigar butt in any public space in Portugal is an offence with a fine of €25 to €250 (Lei 88/2019, arts. 3.º and 11.º). Cafés, restaurants and shops must also put ashtrays and bins outside, and businesses that don't can be fined €250 to €1,500. Your town council may have its own rules on top.
