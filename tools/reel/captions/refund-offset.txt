Finanças can use your IRS refund to pay your tax debts — before the money reaches you.

What the Código de Procedimento e de Processo Tributário says (art. 89.º):

· A refund is applied to debts that the Autoridade Tributária itself collects — not only IRS debts
· If the refund is smaller than the debt, it goes first to default interest, then other charges, then the debt itself
· If the refund is bigger than the debt, the difference is paid to you
· No offset while the deadline to challenge the debt is still running, or while a challenge is open
· No offset while the debt is paid in instalments backed by a guarantee. Instalments without a guarantee do not protect the refund

What to do: check what you owe on the Portal das Finanças before you file, and pay the debt or challenge it in time.

The example in the video (a €800 refund and a €500 debt) is an illustration, not a real case.

Source: CPPT art. 89.º; Portal das Finanças FAQ on IRS refunds.
