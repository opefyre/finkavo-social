Under 35 in Portugal? Year one: zero income tax. 💶🎂 ("Devias ter vindo mais cedo" = "You should have come earlier")

It's called IRS Jovem (Código do IRS, art. 12.º-B). If you're 35 or under and earn employed or self-employed income, part of it is exempt: 100% in your first year, 75% in years 2–4, 50% in years 5–7 and 25% in years 8–10, up to 55 × IAS a year (about €29,500 in 2026). It doesn't apply if you're someone's dependant or have used the NHR regime. Claim it in your IRS return, or tell your employer so less is withheld.
