A free dentist in Portugal? There's a voucher. 🦷🎟️

It's the cheque-dentista, from the national health service (SNS). It's for groups the SNS considers more vulnerable: young people under 18, pregnant women followed in the SNS, people receiving the Complemento Solidário para Idosos, and people with HIV/AIDS, among others. It's usually issued at an appointment at your health centre (for ages 7 and 10, at school), and you use it at a participating dentist anywhere in the country. Source: SNS 24.
