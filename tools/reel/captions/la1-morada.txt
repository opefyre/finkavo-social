Moved flat in Portugal? Do this ONE thing. 📦🏠

If you have a Portuguese citizen card (Cartão de Cidadão), change your address on the card, online or at a counter. That one change reaches the tax office (Finanças), Social Security and the health service (SNS). You'll get a letter with a code: the change only counts once you enter it. You have 60 days. Your car registration and your pet's registry still need their own update.

No citizen card? The rules are different, so check before you move.
