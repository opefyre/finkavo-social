Why do some Portuguese leave dinner out for the dead? 🕯️🎄 ("Isso é do teu avô" = "That's your grandpa's")

In parts of northern Portugal (around Guimarães, Barroso and Porto), the tradition was to leave the Christmas Eve table uncleared so the family's dead, the "alminhas", could come and eat the crumbs. In Alto Minho, some families set an extra place for the one who died most recently. (Source: Ernesto Veiga de Oliveira, Festividades Cíclicas em Portugal.)
