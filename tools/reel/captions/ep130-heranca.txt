Can a Portuguese grandma cut you out of her will? 📜🐈 ("Está decidido" = "It's decided")

No. In Portugal part of an estate is reserved by law for the closest family: with a spouse and children, two-thirds; children alone, half for one child and two-thirds for two or more (Código Civil, art. 2159). Only the rest can go to anyone — even the cat.
