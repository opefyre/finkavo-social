Recibos verdes: getting paid starts a clock, and it's shorter than most people think.

CIVA art. 36.º n.º 1 a) requires the fatura-recibo to be issued within 5 business days of the moment the tax becomes due (art. 7.º/8.º) — that moment is whichever comes first: the service being performed, or payment being received (including a deposit).

This is a general invoicing obligation, not just an IVA one — it applies whether or not you charge IVA, including freelancers under the small-business exemption.

Miss it, and it isn't just a warning: RGIT art. 123.º sets a fine (coima) of €150 to €3.750 for issuing an invoice late or not at all.

What to do: issue the fatura-recibo the same day the money lands, or the same day you finish the service if you haven't been paid yet. Treat the 5-day window as the outer limit, not a plan.

Hedges: periodic or recurring invoicing and intra-EU services may have extended deadlines under the same article — we did not verify those variants against a fully re-read primary text, so we kept this reel to the standard, most common case.

Source: CIVA art. 36.º, 7.º, 8.º; RGIT art. 123.º (Portal das Finanças).
