Why do so many Portuguese streets have this name? 🌹📻 ("Eu estava lá" = "I was there")

25 April 1974. Late that night a song on the radio was the first signal; at 00:20 "Grândola, Vila Morena" was the second. Soldiers moved, people handed them red carnations, and 48 years of dictatorship ended. Today 25 April is a national holiday, and its name is on streets all over the country.
