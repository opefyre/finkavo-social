Got your SNS health number? It might not cover you yet. 🏥🪪

Any foreign resident can get a número de utente, usually at their first visit to a health centre or hospital. But the SNS only covers your costs when the number is linked to your ID, your Portuguese NIF, your Portuguese address and a valid residence permit. Missing one? Sort it out before you need a doctor.
