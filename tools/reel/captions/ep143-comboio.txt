Trains across Portugal for €20 a month? 🚆💶

It's CP's Passe Ferroviário Verde: €20 for 30 days (also €40 for 60 and €60 for 90), for Portuguese citizens and foreigners who live in Portugal. It covers 2nd class on Regional, InterRegional and Intercidades trains, plus the Lisbon, Porto and Coimbra urban trains. Intercidades needs a free seat booking first. It doesn't cover the Alfa Pendular or the special IC-E trains. You load it on a CP card, in the app, online or at a ticket office. Source: cp.pt, checked 11 Nov 2026.
