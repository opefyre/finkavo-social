# EP140 Crypto tax (researched 10 Nov 2026, build after first results)
Hook: "Sell crypto in Portugal after a year? Tax: €0." Score 56/70.
Verified (CIRS, info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt irs10.aspx / irs72.aspx): gains AND losses on crypto held >= 365 days are excluded (art. 10.º, now n.º 22 after DL 97/2026; don't quote paragraph numbers). Exception: not if taxpayer or counterparty is outside EU/EEA / treaty / info-exchange countries. Under 365 days: 28% autonomous rate (art. 72.º n.º 1 c), or opt to aggregate (n.º 13). Crypto-to-crypto swaps not taxed at the swap; acquisition value carries over. NFTs excluded from the definition.
Unverified, keep out: mining / professional trading (category B), reporting duty when exempt.
